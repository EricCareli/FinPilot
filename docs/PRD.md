# FinPilot

## Product Requirements Document

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** In Development

## Product Vision

FinPilot is a personal finance management platform designed to help users organize, understand, and plan their financial lives in one place.

The platform will centralize income, expenses, budgets, and financial goals while providing visual insights and AI-powered analysis.

## Product Goal

The goal of FinPilot is to transform financial data into clear and useful information, helping users make better financial decisions.

## MVP

The initial version will include:

- User authentication
- Financial dashboard
- Income management
- Expense management
- Categories
- Budgets
- Financial goals
- Reports and charts
- AI financial assistant

## Principles

- Security
- Usability
- Scalability
- Maintainability
- Data privacy
